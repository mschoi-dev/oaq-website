Why We Exist

Expanding our senses.
Expanding our understanding.

Technology has long extended human perception. Seeing farther, hearing fainter sounds, and observing beyond the visible have deepened our understanding of the world.

Yet no observation method can fully describe every environment or object. Concealment, limited visibility, and changing weather expose the boundaries of the information we can obtain. OAQ begins by questioning those boundaries.

Is it enough to make our existing senses more powerful?
Could we add a sense we have not yet used?

Why Quantum ISR

Conditions change.
Understanding must continue.

We see a future in which drones and unmanned systems play an expanding role. We expect concealment and protective cover to evolve alongside them. Protecting people and supporting mission continuity in adverse weather and limited visibility calls for a broader approach to observation.

This is why OAQ focuses on Quantum ISR. Our long-term vision extends beyond a single sense or individual sensor: different observations connected to build a wider understanding of a situation.

This is not a guarantee of protection against every threat or complete mission performance in all weather. It is the long-term direction we have chosen to address those limitations.

Our Starting Point

Clues beyond sight.
Starting with magnetic fields.

OAQ starts with magnetic sensing. We explore how magnetic fields can provide another physical source of information about the environment, beyond visible appearance.

Our “sixth sense” is neither intuition nor a supernatural ability. It is a metaphor for extending perception by turning measurable physical quantities into new information. By complementing existing observations, we aim to understand the world more quickly and deeply.
