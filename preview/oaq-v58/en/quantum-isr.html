02 / DATA & AI FLYWHEEL

Every flight is an experiment.
Every mission improves the next.

Deployment produces field data, field data trains our models,
and better models raise accuracy across the most diverse environments.

Building

Deploy → Collect data → Train AI → Improve accuracy

  1. 01

    Mission database

    Every flight is labeled by target, depth, soil, season, terrain and platform.

  2. 02

    AI detection & classification

    Models score anomalies, grade threats and decide re-surveys, lowering false alarms as data grows.

  3. 03

    Magnetic digital twin

    Simulating expected anomalies, coverage and false alarms by terrain and season before flight.
