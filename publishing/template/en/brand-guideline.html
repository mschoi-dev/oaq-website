Brand Values

Integrated. Innovative. Trusted.

Connect. Explore. Build trust.

Integrated

A perspective that connects core components, sensors, and applications.

Innovative

An approach that explores new possibilities through optical and atomic technology.

Trusted

A commitment to building trust through technology and communication.

The three English values come from the original brand document; the accompanying explanations were written for this website.

Logo Concept

Four starting points. One symbol.

Cube, Atom, Sensor, Defense. The logo brings together these four ideas, with a progression described as Cell → Sensor → Defense.

Planned image · Original page 3: Cube, Atom, Sensor, and Defense combined
Planned image · Original page 4: A logomark expanding from Cell to Sensor and Defense

The progression from atomic cells through sensing to wider applications also reflects the direction of OAQ’s business.

Name & Logotype

Atomic precision.
Quantum solution.

These three terms form the OAQ name. Our slogan is “Bridging Invisible to Visible”.
